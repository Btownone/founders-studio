## Testing Protocol

### For Each Funnel:

1. **Show to 16-17 personas** (50 total ÷ 3 funnels ≈ 16-17 per funnel)
2. **Track these metrics:**
   - Did they click the CTA?
   - Did they start the application?
   - Did they complete the application?
   - Which package did they choose?
   - How long did they spend on the page?
   - Did they ask questions?
   - What questions did they ask?

3. **Measure friction points:**
   - Where do they hesitate?
   - What makes them click?
   - What makes them abandon?

### Success Metrics:

- **Funnel A (Baseline):** X% click CTA, Y% complete application, Z% choose $3,500+
- **Funnel B (Soft Tech):** Does it beat Funnel A?
- **Funnel C (Hard Relief):** Does it beat both A and B?

### Key Question:

**Which framing gets them to commit fastest to $3,500-$5,000 packages?**

---

## Important Constraints

✅ **DO:**
- Test ONE variable per funnel (framing)
- Keep copy length consistent
- Keep design consistent
- Measure behavior, not opinions
- Let data decide, not gut feeling

❌ **DON'T:**
- Add extra packages or options
- Explain Atlas or AI deeply
- Mix education language back in
- Get creative without constraints
- Change design between funnels

---

## Expected Outcomes

After testing all 50 personas across 3 funnels, you'll know:

1. **What people anchor on** - Etsy? Relief? Speed?
2. **Where fear shows up** - Checkout? Hardware? Price?
3. **What creates momentum** - Bundling? Guarantees? Proof?
4. **Which framing wins** - Etsy-only? Soft tech? Hard relief?
5. **Which packages convert** - $2,500? $3,500? $5,000?

This data will tell you exactly how to structure the website and offerings for maximum conversion.
