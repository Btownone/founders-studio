## TONE GUIDELINES

**The page should feel:**
- Calm (not hype)
- Reassuring (not pushy)
- Confident (not uncertain)
- Simple (not dense)
- Direct (not flowery)

**If someone reads this and thinks:**
- "This is boring but reassuring" → Perfect
- "This is impressive but dense" → Overshot
- "I'm confused" → Failed

## IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

### For the Developer:
- Keep design minimal and clean
- Use whitespace generously
- One CTA per section (all lead to Apply)
- No distracting elements
- Mobile-first responsive design

### For Testing:
- Measure CTA click rate (target: 90%+)
- Measure application completion (target: 70%+)
- Measure friction level (target: 2.5/5.0 or lower)
- Measure package selection (target: 100% choose Complete or Pro)

### For Iteration:
- If friction is high, remove sections
- If completion is low, simplify copy
- If people choose Starter, re-emphasize Complete/Pro
- If people ask questions, add one line of clarity

---

## SUCCESS CRITERIA

✅ **This copy succeeds if:**
- 90%+ click the CTA
- 70%+ complete the application
- Friction level is 2.5/5.0 or lower
- 100% choose Complete or Pro (not Starter)
- No one asks "What is this?" or "What do I get?"
- People feel confident, not overwhelmed

❌ **This copy fails if:**
- People feel confused
- People ask too many questions
- People abandon during application
- People choose Starter
- The page feels dense or overwhelming
