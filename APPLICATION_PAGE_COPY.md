# Founders Studio - Application Page Copy & Framework

**The qualification gate. Pre-frames commitment. Makes serious people lean in.**

---

## APPLICATION PAGE STRUCTURE

### Section 1: Pre-Frame (Top of Page)

**Headline:**
> "Let's See If This Is a Fit"

**Subheadline:**
> "This isn't for everyone. We review applications to ensure you're ready for what we offer."

**Why this works:**
- Removes "sales pressure" feeling
- Pre-frames selectivity (makes them want to prove they're serious)
- Sets expectation that this is consultative, not transactional

---

### Section 2: What This Means (Reassurance)

**Copy:**

> "Here's what to expect: You'll answer a few questions about your situation, goals, and commitment level. We use this to determine if you're a fit for our system. If you are, we'll schedule a call to discuss specifics. If you're not, we'll be honest about it. Either way, you'll know where you stand."

**Why this works:**
- Removes mystery ("what happens after I apply?")
- Builds trust ("we'll be honest")
- Frames the call as next step, not pressure

---

### Section 3: The Questions (Qualification Filter)

**Structure:** 12-15 questions total. Mix of multiple choice and short text.

**Question Categories:**

#### A. Commitment & Investment (3 questions)

**Q1: Are you willing to invest $3,500–$5,000 to launch your store?**
- [ ] Yes, absolutely
- [ ] Maybe, depends on what's included
- [ ] No, too expensive
- [ ] Not sure

**Q2: How committed are you to following a proven system?**
- [ ] Very committed — I want done-with-you
- [ ] Somewhat committed — I want guidance but some flexibility
- [ ] Not very committed — I prefer to do my own thing

**Q3: What's your timeline?**
- [ ] ASAP (next 30 days)
- [ ] Within 2–3 months
- [ ] Someday, no rush
- [ ] Not sure yet

**Why these matter:**
- Filter out bargain hunters
- Filter out DIY tinkerers
- Identify urgency level

## SCORING SYSTEM (Backend)

### Green Light (Immediate Call)
- Willing to invest ($3,500–$5,000)
- Very committed to system
- Timeline is ASAP or 2–3 months
- Realistic expectations
- Clear motivation

**Auto-response:** "Thanks for applying! We love your energy. [Your name] will reach out within 24 hours to schedule a call."

---

### Yellow Light (Call with Caution)
- Willing to invest but hesitant
- Somewhat committed to system
- Timeline is flexible
- Some concerns but interested
- Needs clarification

**Auto-response:** "Thanks for applying! We think there might be a fit here. [Your name] will reach out within 48 hours to chat about whether this is the right timing."

---

### Red Light (Don't Call)
- Won't invest or unsure
- Wants DIY only
- No urgency
- Unrealistic expectations
- Deal-breaker concerns

**Auto-response:** "Thanks for your interest in Founders Studio. Based on your application, we don't think this is the right fit right now. But we'd love to stay in touch. Reach out if things change!"

## APPLICATION PAGE DESIGN NOTES

**Visual Style:**
- Match the homepage (calm, professional, no hustle energy)
- Use the same color palette (cream, teal, gold)
- Keep form fields clean and spacious
- No progress bars (feels like a long form)
- No pressure language

**Form UX:**
- Questions should feel like a conversation, not an interrogation
- Mix of multiple choice (fast) and short text (depth)
- Keep it to 3–5 minutes to complete
- Mobile-responsive (most will apply on phone)
- Clear "Submit" button (not "Buy" or "Checkout")

---

## WHAT NOT TO DO ❌

- Don't ask for payment info on this page
- Don't ask for too many personal details
- Don't make it feel like a sales form
- Don't use aggressive language ("Limited spots!")
- Don't ask questions you won't use
- Don't make it longer than 5 minutes

---

## COPY FOR SUBMIT BUTTON

**Button text:** "Submit Application"

**Not:** "Buy Now" or "Get Started" or "Apply Now"

**Why:** Reinforces that this is a qualification step, not a purchase.

---

## POST-SUBMISSION FLOW

**Immediately after submission:**

1. **Thank You Page** (see next section)
2. **Email confirmation** (within 1 minute)
3. **Your review** (within 24 hours for Green Light, 48 for Yellow)
4. **Outreach** (call scheduling or rejection)

---

## CALL PREP (For You)

**Before calling, review:**

1. **Their motivation** - Why do they want this?
2. **Their blocker** - What's been holding them back?
3. **Their concern** - What are they worried about?
4. **Their timeline** - When do they need this?
5. **Their commitment** - Are they serious?

**Use this in the call:**

> "I read your application. I loved that you said [their motivation]. And I see your main concern is [their concern]. Let's talk about that."

---

## EXPECTED OUTCOMES

**After 100 applications:**

- 60–70% Green Light (call immediately)
- 20–30% Yellow Light (call with caution)
- 10–20% Red Light (don't call)

**Conversion rate (applications → customers):**
- Green Light: 60–70%
- Yellow Light: 30–40%
- Red Light: 0–5%

## WHAT THIS PAGE ACCOMPLISHES

✅ **Filters out unserious buyers** - DIY tinkerers, bargain hunters, "just curious" people self-select out

✅ **Makes serious people lean in** - Selectivity creates desire

✅ **Surfaces objections early** - You know what to address on the call

✅ **Protects your time** - You only call people who are qualified

✅ **Builds trust** - You're honest about fit, not pushy about sales

✅ **Sets expectations** - They know the next step is a call, not a checkout

---

**This page is the gate. It separates interest from commitment.**

Without it, you're trying to close sales on a landing page. With it, you're qualifying buyers for a conversation.

That's the difference between a sales page and a funnel.
