# Founders Studio - Internal Operations Doc

**One-page reference for every decision.**

---

## WHAT WE ARE

**We are a launch platform that removes the two reasons people quit: technical friction and early discouragement.**

Etsy is the on-ramp. Relief is the product. Momentum is the outcome.

---

## NORTH STAR QUESTION

**Every decision must answer: "Does this reduce the chance they quit?"**

- If yes → do it
- If no → cut it

---

## CORE POSITIONING

**External:** "We give you a working Etsy store — plus the system and support so it actually runs."

**Internal:** AI-powered, system-driven launch platform. Hardware + software + support = no quit.

---

## WEBSITE JOB

**Reduce thinking. Move the right person to apply.**

Not educate. Not impress. Not explain everything.

---

## HOMEPAGE SECTIONS (7 ONLY)

1. **Hero** - "Stop Worrying About Tech. Start Selling on Etsy."

4. **What You Get** - 4 bullets (store, framework, system, guidance)

## HARDWARE STRATEGY

**Core truth:** If their computer can't run the system, they quit and blame us. So we remove that risk.

### What We Install

- Pre-approved laptop (we choose)
- Atlas (browser + locked-in GPT trained on Etsy)
- Image generation access
- Listing optimization workflows
- Automation scripts
- Desktop organized, bookmarks preset, zero config

### What We Don't Do

- Allow "use your old computer" unless approved
- Explain AI to users
- Name tools
- Require configuration

## RED FLAGS (STOP & REASSESS)

- ❌ Explaining AI to prospects
- ❌ Showing hardware specs
- ❌ Mentioning VA services on main page
- ❌ Offering free trials
- ❌ Saying "get rich quick"
- ❌ Complicating the application
- ❌ Adding more than 7 homepage sections
- ❌ Selling to cold traffic directly

---

## GREEN FLAGS (KEEP GOING)

- ✅ Calm, reassuring tone
- ✅ Relief framing (not education)
- ✅ Application required
- ✅ Simple pricing (2 options)
- ✅ Social proof (daily sales, not income claims)
- ✅ Support-focused messaging
- ✅ Hardware invisible to user
- ✅ AI working quietly in background
