# Founders Studio - Sales Call Script

**Discovery call script. Calm, consultative, relief-focused.**

---

## PRE-CALL PREP

**Your mindset:**
- You're not selling. You're qualifying.
- Their commitment matters more than your pitch.
- If they're not ready, that's okay.

**Your goal:**
- Understand their real blocker
- Assess if they're a fit
- Move them to application if yes

---

## OPENING (30 seconds)

> "Hi [Name], thanks for making time. I'm [Your Name] with Founders Studio. Before we dive in, I want to be clear about what this call is — it's not a pitch. It's just a conversation to see if what we're building is a fit for where you are right now. Sound good?"

**Pause. Let them respond.**

---

## DISCOVERY (2-3 minutes)

**Ask one of these, based on their situation:**

### If they're brand new:
> "Walk me through what's holding you back from starting an Etsy store right now. Is it the tech side, or something else?"

### If they've tried before:
> "What happened last time you tried? What made you stop?"

### If they're currently running a store:
> "What's the biggest frustration you have with your store right now?"

**Listen. Don't interrupt. Take notes.**

---

## VALIDATE THEIR PAIN (30 seconds)

**Mirror back what you heard:**

> "So it sounds like [their blocker] is the main thing. That's what we hear from almost everyone. You're not alone in that."

**This builds trust. You're not selling. You're validating.**

---

## INTRODUCE THE SOLUTION (1 minute)

**Keep it simple:**

> "Here's what we do differently. We don't teach you Etsy. We build you a working Etsy store — with the system, the tools, and the support so it actually runs. You don't have to figure out the tech. You don't have to debug anything. You just focus on selling."

**Pause. Let that land.**

> "The reason we do it this way is because we've seen that people quit Etsy for two reasons: tech breaks down, or they don't see momentum fast enough. We eliminate both."

---

## QUALIFY THEIR COMMITMENT (1-2 minutes)

**Ask these in order:**

### Question 1: Willingness to invest
> "This is a $3,500 to $5,000 investment. Is that something you'd be willing to do if this felt like the right fit?"

**If no:** "That's totally fair. This might not be the right timing. Let's stay in touch."

**If yes:** Continue.

### Question 2: Willingness to follow the system
> "We give you a pre-built system. The key is following it. Are you someone who can commit to that, or do you prefer to do things your own way?"

**If "my own way":** "That's great. You might be better served with a course. This is really for people who want done-with-you."

**If "I can follow it":** Continue.

### Question 3: Timeline
> "When would you want to have your store live and making sales?"

**If "ASAP":** "Perfect. That's exactly what we do."

**If "someday":** "That's cool. We're not going anywhere. Let's stay in touch."

---

## DESCRIBE THE EXPERIENCE (2 minutes)

**Paint the picture, not the specs:**

> "Here's what happens. You apply. We review your application — we're looking for commitment, not perfection. If it's a fit, we get you set up within 48 hours."

> "Day one, your store exists. Everything's already configured. You don't have to think about tech. You just start building and selling."

> "Within the first week or two, you'll see your first activity. That's when it gets real. You start seeing momentum."

> "Throughout all of this, you have us. If you get stuck, you ask. We unblock you. That's it."

**Pause.**

> "Does that feel like what you need?"

---

## ADDRESS OBJECTIONS

### Objection: "I'm not sure if I can do this"

> "That's normal. Here's the thing — you don't have to do this alone. That's why we're here. Your job is to follow the system and stay consistent. Our job is to make sure nothing breaks. You're not figuring this out by yourself."

### Objection: "What if it doesn't work?"

> "That's a fair question. Here's what I'll say — we've built multiple stores using this exact system. They're under 6 months old and already generating daily sales. We're not asking you to do something we haven't done ourselves. That said, results depend on execution. We can't guarantee outcomes, but we can guarantee you won't get stuck on tech."

### Objection: "I need to think about it"

> "That's totally fair. Here's what I'd suggest — apply anyway. The application is just a conversation. It doesn't commit you to anything. It just gets us talking about whether this is the right fit. Sound good?"

### Objection: "Can I do this with my current computer?"

> "Maybe. It depends on what you're working with. If your current setup can handle modern AI tools and multitasking without lag, you're probably fine. If not, we can help you with that. But we don't want you stuck on tech, so we make sure your setup is solid before you start."

---

## CLOSE (1 minute)

### If they're ready:
> "Great. Here's the next step. You're going to fill out a short application. It's just so we understand what you're trying to build and make sure we can help. Then we'll get you set up within 48 hours. Sound good?"

### If they're not sure:
> "No pressure. Here's what I'd say — apply anyway. It's just a conversation. Worst case, you learn more about what we do. Best case, you're on your way to launching your store. What do you think?"

### If they're not a fit:
> "I appreciate your time. I don't think this is the right fit for where you are right now, and I want to be honest about that. But let's stay in touch. If things change, reach out."

---

## POST-CALL

**Send within 1 hour:**

> "Hi [Name], thanks again for the call. I loved learning about [their goal]. Here's the application link: [link]. Fill it out when you get a chance, and we'll take it from there. Any questions, just reply to this email."

---

## CALL METRICS TO TRACK

- **Application rate:** % of calls that lead to application
- **Application completion:** % of applications completed
- **Conversion rate:** % of applications → customers
- **Average call length:** Should be 10–15 minutes
- **Objection patterns:** What do people ask most?

---

## TONE REMINDERS

- ✅ Calm, not pushy
- ✅ Consultative, not salesy
- ✅ Validating, not dismissive
- ✅ Clear, not complicated
- ✅ Honest, not hype

---

## RED FLAGS (Don't work with them)

- They want "get rich quick"
- They won't commit to the system
- They're not willing to invest
- They're looking for free or cheap
- They want to build everything from scratch

---

## GREEN FLAGS (They're a fit)

- They're frustrated with tech
- They want done-with-you, not DIY
- They're willing to invest
- They want results fast
- They'll follow a system

---

**Remember:** You're not selling. You're qualifying. If they're not ready, that's okay. You're looking for people who are ready to stop worrying about tech and start selling.
