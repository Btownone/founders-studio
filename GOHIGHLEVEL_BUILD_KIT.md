## 📝 Page 2: Application Form (The Gate)

Create a Form in GHL named "Founders Application" with these fields:

1.  **First Name**
2.  **Last Name**
3.  **Email**
4.  **Phone**
5.  **Custom Field: Current Income** (Label: "What is your current monthly income?")
6.  **Custom Field: Investment Budget** (Label: "Do you have capital to invest in your business setup?")
    *   *Options:* Yes ($3k-$5k), Yes ($5k+), No (I need financing), No (I have $0)
7.  **Custom Field: Biggest Struggle** (Label: "What has stopped you from starting before?")
8.  **Custom Field: Why Now** (Label: "Why is now the right time to launch?")
