# Founders Studio: High-Ticket Offer & Lead Magnet Strategy

To sell a $2,000+ product, we must move from selling "information" to selling "implementation and results."

## 1. The Lead Magnet: "The 2025 Etsy POD Blueprint"
**Goal**: Capture emails and establish authority.
**Format**: A 10-page PDF or a 15-minute video training.
**Core Hook**: "How to launch a profitable Etsy store in 30 days using AI and proven systems—without inventory or tech overwhelm."

### Key Sections:
- **The Opportunity**: Why Etsy POD is the best asset to own in 2025.
- **The AI Advantage**: How we use proprietary tools to skip 90% of the manual work.
- **The 3-Step Framework**: Setup -> Optimize -> Scale.
- **The Case Study**: A real-world example of a store reaching $5k/mo.

## 2. The "Value Ladder" Offer Structure

| Tier | Name | Price | Core Value Proposition | Key Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bait** | The Blueprint | FREE | Instant Value & Trust | PDF Guide + Video Training |
| **Front-End** | Catalyst | $1,997 | The Foundation | DFY Store Setup + 12 Modules + Community |
| **Middle** | Accelerator | $2,997 | The Speed & Support | Everything in Catalyst + AI Tools + 1-on-1 Call |
| **Back-End** | Formula | Custom | The Partnership | Ongoing Coaching + Custom Growth Strategy |

## 3. The "High-Ticket" Positioning
We are not selling a "course." We are selling a **Business-in-a-Box**.

### Refined Messaging:
- **Instead of**: "Learn Etsy POD."
- **Use**: "Own a Profitable Digital Asset."
- **Instead of**: "12 Modules of Training."
- **Use**: "The Proven System for $10k/mo."
- **Instead of**: "Support Team."
- **Use**: "Your Dedicated Operational Partners."

## 4. Risk Reversal & Trust Elements
- **The "Done-For-You" Guarantee**: We don't just teach; we build the foundation for you.
- **Lifetime Ownership**: You own the store, the customers, and the skills forever.
- **Exclusivity**: "We only onboard 5 new founders per month to ensure quality support."
