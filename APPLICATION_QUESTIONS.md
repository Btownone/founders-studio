## SECTION 3: COMMITMENT (1 minute)

- [ ] Yes, absolutely

- [ ] No, too expensive

- [ ] Very committed — I want done-with-you

**If "do my own thing":** Auto-reply: "Thanks for applying. This program is for people who want done-with-you. You might be better served with a course. Good luck!"

## SCORING SYSTEM

**Green Light (Move to call):**
- Willing to invest ($3,500–$5,000)
- Wants done-with-you (not DIY)
- Timeline is ASAP or 2–3 months
- Seems committed

**Yellow Light (Call with caution):**
- Willing to invest but hesitant
- Wants some flexibility
- Timeline is "someday"
- Has concerns but interested

**Red Light (Don't call):**
- Won't invest
- Wants DIY only
- No urgency
- Unrealistic expectations

---

## AUTO-RESPONSES

### Green Light:
> "Thanks for applying! We love your energy. [Your name] will reach out within 24 hours to schedule a quick call. See you soon!"

### Yellow Light:
> "Thanks for applying! We think there might be a fit here. [Your name] will reach out within 48 hours to chat about whether this is the right timing. Talk soon!"

### Red Light:
> "Thanks for your interest in Founders Studio. Based on your application, we don't think this is the right fit right now. But we'd love to stay in touch. Reach out if things change!"

---

## CALL PREP (For Green/Yellow)

**Before calling, review:**

1. **Their motivation** - Why do they want this?
2. **Their blocker** - What's been holding them back?
3. **Their concern** - What are they worried about?
4. **Their timeline** - When do they need this?

**Use this in the call:**

> "I read your application. I loved that you said [their motivation]. And I see your main concern is [their concern]. Let's talk about that."

---

## FOLLOW-UP SEQUENCE

**If they don't apply within 3 days:**
- Email 1: "Checking in — any questions?"

**If they apply but don't answer call:**
- Text/Email 1: "Got your application. Trying to reach you for a quick call."
- Text/Email 2 (24h later): "Still trying to connect. Let me know what works."

**If they go silent after call:**
- Email 1 (24h): "Great talking with you. Here's the next step..."
- Email 2 (3 days): "Still interested? Let me know."
- Email 3 (1 week): "Last check-in. Let's get you launched."

---

## WHAT NOT TO ASK

❌ Income claims ("How much will I make?")
❌ Guarantees ("Will this definitely work?")
❌ Detailed tech questions ("What software do you use?")
❌ Long-form essays (keep it short)
❌ Personal details (age, background, etc.)

---

## WHAT TO MEASURE

- **Application completion rate** - % who start and finish
- **Green light rate** - % of applications that are qualified
- **Call show-up rate** - % who take the call
- **Conversion rate** - % of calls → customers
- **Average time to complete** - Should be 3–5 minutes

---

## FINAL NOTES

**Keep it short.** People should complete this in 3–5 minutes.

**Keep it clear.** No jargon. Simple language.

**Keep it honest.** No tricks. No false urgency. Just straightforward questions.

**Use the answers.** Reference their application in the call. Show you listened.

---

**This application is a commitment filter, not a sales tool.**

If they won't fill it out, they're not ready.
If they fill it out, they're signaling interest.
Your job is to validate that interest on the call.
