# Founders Studio - Funnel Test Results & Scaling Strategy

## Executive Summary

**🎯 CLEAR WINNER: Funnel B (Soft Tech Relief)**

After testing 50 personas across 3 funnel variants, the data is unambiguous: **"Everything is already set up so you don't get stuck"** (Soft Tech Relief) drives the fastest conversion to $3,500-$5,000 packages.

**Key Results:**
- **100% CTA click rate** (vs. 71% baseline, 94% hard relief)
- **71% application completion rate** (vs. 18% baseline, 56% hard relief)
- **Lowest friction level** (2.4/5.0 vs. 3.5 baseline, 3.0 hard relief)
- **$4,125 average package value** (vs. $3,500 baseline, $4,167 hard relief)

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Relief Framing Eliminates Core Anxiety

**Data:** In Funnel B, 24% of personas had NO objections. In Funnels A and C, this number was 0%.

**Implication:** The relief framing ("Stop Worrying About Tech") directly addresses the core anxiety that prevents action. When you eliminate that anxiety, personas move forward without hesitation.

### Finding 2: Bundling Increases Decision Fatigue

**Data:** Funnel C had 75% asking questions vs. 59% in Funnels A and B. Completion rate dropped from 71% to 56%.

**Implication:** Too many options (hardware, software, support, training) creates decision paralysis. Personas spend more time thinking and less time committing.

### Finding 3: Commitment Objection is the Real Barrier

**Data:** Commitment was the #1 objection in all funnels (35-69%). Price was secondary (24-29%).

**Implication:** Personas aren't hesitating because of price. They're hesitating because they're unsure if they can commit to this. Relief framing reduces this objection from 69% to 35%.

### Finding 4: $3,500-$5,000 is the Right Price Point

**Data:** In Funnel B, 58% chose Complete ($3,500) and 42% chose Pro ($5,000). Zero chose Starter ($2,500).

**Implication:** Your personas are willing to pay premium prices. They're not price-sensitive—they're commitment-sensitive. When you reduce commitment anxiety, they choose higher-value packages.

### Finding 5: Soft Tech Framing Beats Hard Relief

**Data:** Funnel B completion rate (71%) > Funnel C completion rate (56%).

**Implication:** "Everything is set up so you don't get stuck" is more powerful than "we remove the #1 reason people quit." The former is about relief. The latter is about solving a problem. Relief is more motivating than problem-solving.
